Support

ParkMate is built and supported by one person, so replies are quick but not instant.

Found a bug, spotted a missed detection, or want something added? Send it through the form or email hello@bhapps.co.uk. For detection problems, the date, time and rough location make a report much easier to act on.

Early access

ParkMate is in closed testing on Google Play. Use the form below with Early access / beta request as the topic and include the Google account email you use on your Android phone; you'll be added to the tester list and sent the install link.

Contact form

Delete your data

ParkMate keeps nothing about you on a server. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone. See the privacy policy for details.
